
**An online department store management system could be used by management staff, sale staff, and customer:

* Management staff: view all kinds of statistics: imports, exports, customers and suppliers. Commodity information management. Import goods from suppliers, manage supplier information
* Sale staff: browse invoices online, pack and deliver to delivery staff
* Customer: register as a member, find and look up product information, order online, track invoice status.

Considering two  modules and answering questions

* Customer tracks invoice: selects the invoice tracking menu → clicks on the invoice from the list of ordered invoices → the system displays detailed information and current status of the invoice.
* Sale staff approves orders + export: selects menu to browse orders → selects unexported orders in the list → selects delivery staff and update the status of exported order → prints invoices and deliver goods + invoices for the delivery staff .
